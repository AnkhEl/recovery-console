# Coordination simulator v3: mapped obstruction sight lines

`coordination-simulator-v3` adds a deterministic, two-dimensional building-footprint obstruction check to the v2 simulated unit-observation rule. It does not use real telemetry, predict a vehicle route, train a model, or enable AI-generated advice.

## Footprint audit before use

The frozen local extract in `src/data/sight-line-footprints.v1.json` came from OpenStreetMap XML for bbox `28.045,-26.107,28.052,-26.1025`, retrieved 2026-09-28 06:17:43 UTC. `scripts/extract-sight-line-footprints.py` records the transformation. The source is © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL; the map keeps attribution. No browser call to OSM, API key, backend, or recurring footprint service is used.

The source XML had 1,799 nodes, 185 ways, and 17 relations. Thirty-four ways carried `building=*` (30 `yes`, four `commercial`). All 34 were closed, had their referenced nodes, and passed the extract's ground-level filter; there were no building-tagged relations in this extract. A separate geometric audit found no zero-area or self-crossing ring. These checks validate the stored polygons' basic structure, **not completeness or real-world visibility**. In particular, the extract has no mapped building vertices within 120 m of Grayston Drive × Sandton Drive. Coverage near other junctions also varies. Missing structures, walls, vegetation, terrain, openings, and building heights remain unknown. Every simulated observation that passes the footprint check is labelled **“No mapped blockage; actual visibility uncertain.”**

All 21 configured visible incoming directed edges were checked against the extract. None intersects a stored footprint when the observation point is the existing junction node. This follows from the current straight-edge geometry: the unit-to-vehicle ray lies on the incoming edge. Thus this particular OSM extract does not suppress a sighting in the ordinary base-graph scenarios. That is a geometry/data limitation, not evidence that visibility is clear. The on-map footprint toggle allows inspection of the incomplete mapped data.

## Observation rule and information boundary

V3 retains v2's order, stationary post, three-second dwell, designated incoming directed approach (including a short edge completed within one tick), 75 m range, seeded visibility state, per-traversal deduplication, and two-second availability delay. After those conditions, a closed ground-level mapped footprint crossing the simulated segment from the posted sight point to the vehicle's internal position blocks that attempt. This check is pure and replay deterministic. It does not create a sighting merely because a vehicle passes near a unit.

The synthetic vehicle position is used only inside the observation calculation. The operator sees static footprints and a unit observation **after availability**, never the hidden ray or vehicle position during GPS loss. A unit observation remains separate from a vehicle device report and does not advance the last confirmed GPS marker or its heading arrow. Blocked attempts are not inserted into incident activity, since that would leak hidden vehicle movement.

The browser-only fixture `?scenarioSeed=27&sightLineFixture=clear` and matching `blocked` variant use the same synthetic Unit 02 sight point near Fredman Drive × Sandown Valley Crescent. Only `blocked` adds a visibly labelled *synthetic* corner footprint between the sight point and the configured approach. This controlled geometry demonstrates the new rule without misrepresenting the OSM extract as having a blocking building there. In both variants, assign Unit 02 to that junction at 00:00. At 01:03, the clear fixture shows a unit observation made at 01:01 during the scheduled GPS outage; the blocked fixture has no such observation. Both keep vehicle report `vehicle-55` as last confirmed at 01:03. At 03:00 the clear fixture also has a later observation; the blocked fixture suppresses it.

## V2 versus v3 and limits

On the frozen OSM base graph, the sight-line audit proves **zero observation changes** for any run using the existing straight incoming edges and junction-node sight points. The controlled seed 27 fixture changes the 01:01 observation from present under the clear/v2-equivalent geometry to absent under the synthetic mapped obstruction, with all movement, traffic, and vehicle branching unchanged. This is a simulator geometry comparison, **not a model result or evaluation of recovery performance**. Historical `observation-ranker-v3` weights and reports remain results from the earlier simulator. No model was trained or assessed against v3, and AI-generated options stay disabled under the existing display gate.

This version treats stored ground-level building footprints as opaque in 2D. The default post is the graph junction node, with no unit gaze limit. It does not model height, windows, overpasses, terrain, vegetation, street furniture, or unmapped structures. A clear line against an incomplete extract is an uncertain simulated visibility condition, never verified real-world intervisibility. A future change to post placement or curved road geometry would need another version and fresh paired evaluation.
