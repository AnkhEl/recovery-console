## Frozen views and policy inputs

- For each of the 32 seeds, evaluate decision seconds **0, 35, 65, 95** in two separately reported streams: (1) the original road graph with congestion `seed % 2` and outage pattern `floor(seed / 2) % 2`; (2) alternate graph layout `2 + seed % 2`, congestion `2 + seed % 2`, and outage `2 + floor(seed / 2) % 2`. Layout and outage construction use the existing synthetic ranking experiment formulas. There are **128 views per stream**, 256 total before eligibility filtering.
- The run uses the existing seed-based prior-order formula (`seed % 3`), current simulated traffic and outage policies, and the existing 180-second replay. Decision policies receive only `rankingCandidates` from the same operator-visible snapshot and its current unit orders, graph geometry, and traffic. Hidden vehicle position, branch scores, future traffic and reports, and counterfactual outcomes are never policy inputs.
- The shared candidate pool contains only finite legal-route candidates passing the existing confirmed-report freshness checks and the team coverage earliest-availability timing check: `ceil(decision second + current-traffic route seconds + 3) + 2 <= min(decision second + 90, 180)`. This is a possibility filter, not a predicted sighting. Both two-order policies have the same maximum of two new orders, with distinct units, at the decision second; if fewer distinct units qualify, both use that smaller budget. Orders are deterministic and use the same prior actions.
- Arms: **no new order**; **single-order baseline** (highest existing transparent `baselineScore`); **equal-budget baseline** (top two candidates by that score for distinct units); **team combination** (enumerate pairs of distinct units, maximize the change in the union of configured approaches covered by fresh confirmed incumbent posts plus the proposed objectives, accounting for posts vacated by retasks, then break ties by summed baseline score and stable IDs). When only one unit qualifies, both two-order arms use the same highest-scored single candidate. The team policy may select an exploratory combination for this offline test; no browser advice is enabled by it.
- An incumbent contributes configured approaches to that union only when its latest unit report is confirmed, has valid device/receipt chronology, is no older than 10 seconds at display time, and lies within 20 m of its ordered junction. This is evidence of a reported position, not proof of uninterrupted dwell or a future vehicle approach. The objective sets are restricted to directed edges present in the study graph.

## Frozen outcomes and analysis

- For each arm, replay its action log to `min(decision second + 90, 180)`. A hit requires a valid simulated **unit observation** with `observedSecond > decision second` and `availableSecond <= endpoint`, from any unit including incumbents. The no-order arm uses the identical pre-decision run. This avoids credit for observations already made but waiting through the two-second availability delay.
- Primary results: paired overall hit rates, plus paired **incremental effects versus no new order**, each using the same eligible-decision denominator. Report all-view rates and suppression counts separately. Record actual added orders per arm, first available observation time, and the count of distinct observed junctions. The principal combination comparison is against the equal-budget baseline; the single-order arm remains a reference.
- Offline oracle feasibility enumerates the same legal singles and distinct-unit pairs (and the no-order arm) only after policy choices are frozen. Report no-order hits, decisions where any additional order can create a hit absent under no order, and decisions where no legal combination can succeed. Oracle outcomes never feed a policy.
- Report representative and alternate streams separately, and connected versus GPS-loss decision views separately. Use paired 95% percentile bootstrap intervals resampling **whole seed families** (5,000 fixed-seed draws) for policy differences and incremental effects. Include cases where the two two-order policies choose different orders, showing each policy's actions and outcomes. Report unfavorable or inconclusive results without tuning on these seeds.
- Keep `coordination-simulator-v2`, observation rules, transparent baseline formula, model artifacts, AI display gate, and browser suggestions unchanged. Improved intervisibility and line-of-sight geometry are a later, separately versioned simulator limitation; this study does not alter them.
